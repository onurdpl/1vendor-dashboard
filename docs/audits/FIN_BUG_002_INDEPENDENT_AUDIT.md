## 13. Potential risks and test gaps

- **R-1 · HIGH potential financial exposure · POTENTIAL_RISK / TEST_GAP, separate scope:** A vendor may have a PENDING adjustment associated with one approval while an unrelated approved settlement remains payout-selectable. Therefore “the very next payout must absorb the outstanding refund” is not proved. This is an intentional local payout-selection comment, not proof of money loss or duplicate deduction. Validate business obligation and a real cross-approval PostgreSQL scenario before deciding whether a global hold/debt conversion is required; do not silently weaken the same-approval hold. [`finance:2777-2795,2888-2920`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/finance.service.ts), [`schema:2064-2137`](../../backend/prisma/schema.prisma).

Existing independent approval revalidation and source authority may cover relevant drift, but complete metadata-equivalence is not proved from this comparison alone. Add focused drift characterization before any change; do not reject legitimate non-economic metadata differences by assumption. [`assessment:41-51,223-239`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/settlement-replacement-assessment.service.ts), [`approval:2630-2675`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/settlement-approval.service.ts).
