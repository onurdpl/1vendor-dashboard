## Authority and fixture

Three focused cases were added to the existing guarded [`fin-bug-002-cancelled-cycle.postgres.test.ts`](../../src/fin-bug-002-cancelled-cycle.postgres.test.ts). The suite's existing fixture creates a vendor, frozen 0-day Kargonomi outbound source, matching synthetic ShipmentExecution, and canonical `recordVerifiedDeliveredObservation`; the SALE becomes eligible through existing services. The refund is accepted by `ingestVerifiedShopifyRefund`, which persists an exact RefundEvidenceSnapshot/REFUND ledger and invokes existing adjustment creation for an approved SALE. The synthetic accepted evidence is local to the disposable database; no live Shopify or carrier call occurs. See test helper lines 77-248 and [`refund-ingestion.service.ts:1335-1398`](../../backend/src/modules/shopify/refund-ingestion.service.ts).

Approval A contains only its own 200.00 TRY SALE: 20,000 gross minor units minus 2,000 commission and 400 commission VAT = **17,600 payable minor units**. A later accepted 10.00 TRY refund has **880 vendor-side reversal minor units** (not the 1,000 gross refund). Its adjustment is uniquely linked to the exact REFUND ledger, RefundRecord and approval A; it starts `PENDING`, `original=880`, `applied=0`, `remaining=880`. The separate B SALE is created and APPROVED **before** the refund is accepted. This chronology matters: creating a new vendor-wide DRAFT after the refund would ordinarily apply the pending adjustment, so doing that would not characterize an already-approved independent B. [`settlement-approval.service.ts:2138-2145,2334-2398`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/settlement-approval.service.ts), [`settlement-refund-adjustment.service.ts:414-577`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/settlement-refund-adjustment.service.ts).

## Financial interpretation and limits

This is a **confirmed payout-sequencing behavior**, not by itself a confirmed production defect or proof that the refund is permanently lost. The code explicitly holds the affected approval but leaves unrelated approvals selectable. When B is paid, A's **880 minor-unit** obligation remains durable and unapplied; a later qualifying settlement C can consume it. The maximum temporary unoffset vendor-side amount demonstrated by the 100.00 TRY B fixture is **880 minor units**, while the B payout itself is 8,800 minor units. The smaller-B fixture demonstrates 440 minor units can reach REVIEW while 880 remains pending. These are synthetic test amounts, not production exposure estimates. If no later eligible settlement exists, *when or whether* the pending amount is recovered is `UNKNOWN`; a PENDING adjustment must not be described as cash already deducted. [`finance.service.ts:2777-2795`](../../backend/src/modules/finance/finance.service.ts), [settlement model](../FINANCE_SETTLEMENT_MODEL.md).

No existing approved rule identified in [the finance register](../FINANCIAL_BUGS.md) or [settlement model](../FINANCE_SETTLEMENT_MODEL.md) mandates a vendor-wide hold or deduction from the next unrelated payout. Whether to impose one, how to handle a payout smaller than the obligation, and what to do when no future settlement arrives are **product/financial decisions**. Do not silently turn PENDING into vendor debt, add a global payout hold, or alter PAID history. The smallest justified next step is a separate decision on desired recovery timing and authority, not a production code patch inferred from these tests. FIN-BUG-002 remains CLOSED.
